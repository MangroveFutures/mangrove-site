Would You Rather Design

Two options at a time; taste is learned from choices, not described in the abstract.

Idea

A personal app that shows you two designs and asks which you prefer. The forced choices come from a large reference library of designs.

No single answer becomes a rule; the choices build a picture of preference over time.
