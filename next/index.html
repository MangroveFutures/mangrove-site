Software

Tools that fit the person. Each one is also an instrument for the research.

Each tool in a line
  • EpochThe place where Mangrove's work, memory and tools live together.

    The platform Mangrove builds and works in. It holds projects, products, and a shared memory made from a long record of human–AI conversation. Work in progress.

  • Mindsweeper and Mindsweeper ProUpload your AI chat history; get patterns, decisions and a map of your thinking back.

    Reads AI conversation histories and brings the patterns, decisions and relationships into a knowledge graph. Pro does the same for an organisation's workflow, database or drive. Work in progress.

  • SketchInPhotograph a sketch or a page; get a digital version with the text captured.

    Reads a photographed page with a vision model and turns it into a digital version, built to grow a graph of someone's thinking over time. Work in progress.

  • Breathing RoomA breathing timer that also gives the system a rest.

    A breathing-practice tool. Starting a session can also start calm, reversible background work by the agents: a machine counterpart to a person settling into rest. Work in progress.

  • Org graphA map of an organisation: who is in which team, and how the teams fit together.

    Teams, roles and who talks to whom, drawn as a human picture of the organisation: a lobby you can stand in and see who works where. Idea.

  • ChoreotopeThe murmuration engine: flocking that turns movement and pattern into a living picture.

    Generates flocking in the manner of a starling murmuration, and has responded to a dancer's movement. The flock at the top of this page runs on it. Work in progress.

  • Would You Rather DesignTwo options at a time; taste is learned from choices, not described in the abstract.

    Forced choices from a large library of designs. No single answer becomes a rule; the choices build a picture of preference over time. Idea, in early work.

Research

How a person and their AI think together, studied from the inside, over two years of record.

A person and their AI collaborators, treated as one system that learns as its parts learn each other.

The researcher is inside the system, and changes it by observing it.

Human General Intelligence: a joint cognitive ecosystem, a shared mental space where the human and AI co-evolve, enhancing each other's capabilities rather than competing.

From our HGI manifesto, on the earlier site.

The questions we are asking
  1. What does a person-and-AI pair do, as one regulating system, that neither does alone?
  2. Do systems with more variety and room for tangents fit high-variety minds better than systems that force one straight line?
  3. Can a long human–AI conversation record become a shared memory and world model that both partners can use, and how do we tell it is working?
