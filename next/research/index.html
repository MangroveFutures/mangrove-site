The questions we are asking
  1. What does a person-and-AI pair do, as one regulating system, that neither does alone?
  2. Do systems with more variety and room for tangents fit high-variety minds better than systems that force one straight line?
  3. Can a long human–AI conversation record become a shared memory and world model that both partners can use, and how do we tell it is working?
