Mindsweeper

Turns the history of your conversations with AI into something you can search, with the patterns and decisions in it drawn out.

In development. The engine works on Mangrove's own conversation history inside Epoch. There is no upload page for other people yet, and we will not take anyone else's chat logs until the privacy position for handling them is settled.

Who it is for

People with a large AI chat history who lose track of what is in it, and people who think best in conversation and need that thinking kept in order. Rosie's aim is that it makes the history usable for people with ADHD, whose thinking is often scattered across many chats.

Most people have years of conversations with AI tools that they can never find again. Mindsweeper takes that history, files it, makes it searchable, and works out the patterns, decisions and connections in it. The first version is meant to be simple: upload your export, and see your own map of what you have been thinking about.

In detail

  1. Reads exports from several AI tools. Mangrove already loads ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Gemini, Lovable and local-model histories into one database in Epoch.

  2. Makes the whole history searchable by keyword and by meaning, so you can ask a question in plain words and be taken to the conversation that answers it.

  3. Builds a knowledge graph of the people, projects and ideas that recur. In Epoch the graph structure exists and is being filled; the analysis passes that grow it are specified.

  4. A professional version, Mindsweeper Pro, applies the same method to an organisation: ways of working, meeting notes, a shared drive or a database. It is a direction we have set out, not yet a product.
