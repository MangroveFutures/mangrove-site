The argument

Advice to slow down, to focus on fewer things, to cut the tangents, is for some people fundamentally wrong. A mind that wants to move quickly or non-linearly feels throttled when it is forced to move slowly or in artificially rigid ways, just as a linear thinker would hate a workflow designed by someone whose mind works very differently.

The answer is to have people who understand that way of thinking build systems for it: systems that follow the natural inclinations of the mind, and make practical use of AI where it helps. A tangent is often the route to a connection across fields that a straight line never reaches.
