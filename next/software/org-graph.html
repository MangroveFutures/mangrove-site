Org graph

A map of an organisation: who is in which team, and how the teams fit together.

Idea

The org graph shows teams, roles, who talks to whom and in what order, drawn as a human picture of the organisation. It is meant to become a lobby you can stand in and see who works where.

It also holds who owns what, with producers and consumers mapped.
