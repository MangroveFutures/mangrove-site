Software

Software we build and use ourselves. Each product starts as something Mangrove needed for its own work, and each one is described here as it stands today: what already works, and what is still being designed.

  1. Epoch

    The platform Mangrove works in: one searchable record of our conversations, projects and people, with named AI agents working on it.

    In use internally

  2. Mindsweeper

    Turns the history of your conversations with AI into something you can search, with the patterns and decisions in it drawn out.

    In development

  3. SketchIn

    A findable record of every design, sketch and page you have made: each one given an ID, a snapshot, a written description and its links to the others.

    In developmentOpen question. The old card said: photograph a sketch and get text. The repository is a design-catalogue pipeline. Which one is the product is Rosie's call. Review note, not page copy.

  4. Breathing Room

    A breathing practice with a guided timer, water sound and a slow flock to watch. It records each session, and is meant to ask the AI agents to rest while you do.

    In development

  5. Org graph

    A map of an organisation drawn from its own records: who owns what and how the parts connect.

    Research prototype

  6. Choreotope

    A flock of birds you can steer with a phone, a camera or music, and a tool for writing dance movement down and watching it performed.

    In development

  7. Would You Rather Design

    A way of finding out what you like in design by choosing between two options, marking up parts of a picture and saying why. Every answer is kept as a record of taste.

    In development

Health

  1. HealthStory AI

    Your health story, in your words and in order, kept as a record you own and can turn into whatever a doctor, a family member or a researcher needs.

    In design

  2. BodyScan

    A guided walk through the body, toe to head, for noticing where and how things feel, and a clear summary you can take to an appointment.

    Research prototype
