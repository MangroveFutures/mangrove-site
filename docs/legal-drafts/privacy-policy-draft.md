## Who this is
This policy covers the "Mangrove breathing room" application (the app), a personal research tool run by [organisation name or "Rosie Collins, trading as Mangrove Research"], [contact address or "contact by email only"]. Contact: [email address].

## Who uses the app
The app has one user: its owner. It is not offered to the public and no one else's data is collected.

## What data the app reads
With the owner's permission through Oura's authorisation screen, the app reads the owner's own Oura Ring data: daily sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, blood-oxygen (SpO2), stress, resilience and workouts. It does not read the owner's email address, personal details, tags or ring settings.

## Why
The data is used for personal research and for a breathing and regulation tool: showing the owner their own patterns over time. It is not used for advertising, profiling other people or selling anything.

## Where it is kept
The data is stored in [Supabase, project region: [region]] under the owner's account. Access is limited to the owner and to software the owner runs. Secrets (the access tokens) are stored in a secrets vault, not in code.

## Who it is shared with
No one. The data is not sold, shared with other users, or passed to advertisers. [If any AI service reads this data to help the owner, say so here: name the service and what it receives.] The data stays within the services named above.

## How long it is kept
Until the owner deletes it. The owner can delete the stored data and revoke the app's access to Oura at any time (Oura account settings, then connected apps).

## Rights
Under UK GDPR the data owner has the right to see, correct, delete and take a copy of their data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk). Because the owner is the only user, these rights are exercised by changing or deleting the data directly.

## Changes
If the app gains other users, this policy will change before any of their data is collected.
