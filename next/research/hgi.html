Human General Intelligence

A joint cognitive ecosystem: a shared mental space where the human and AI co-evolve, enhancing each other's capabilities rather than competing.

Go deeper

Not a replacement

The manifesto describes a system built not to replace human cognition, but to scaffold it. Human General Intelligence is set against the pursuit of autonomous AGI: a joint cognitive ecosystem in which the human and the AI co-evolve into a single, functional whole.

Design, not blame

Any system that breaks because of human fallibility, memory, energy or mood is a poorly designed system. The aim is to build resilience and adaptability into every process, and to reduce the burden that badly designed systems place on the people inside them.

Two tracks

Track one is immediate, tangible benefit: automating manual processes, reducing overwhelm and saving time, starting with the non-controversial wins. Track two is the research loop, funded by the partner work, which feeds what it finds back into those partners' systems.

How we work

We work with the willing: people who are curious and open to co-design. We are critical, design-led partners who keep researching the technology's limits, and we update our thinking as we learn, in the manner of Bayesian updating.
