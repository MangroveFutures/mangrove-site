Mangrove is a living speculative design lab. Every tool, product, and story it generates is a prototype of a wider possibility: part of a modular ecosystem that reimagines what personal infrastructure, research, and collaboration could feel like if they were designed for real human rhythm.

The flagship system, Epoch OS, and our central Mangrove Protocol, are the most tangible results but they only exist because of the sprawling experimental landscape that came before it: logs, somatic research, frameworks, fiction, hybrid rituals, AI experiments. Speculative design in Mangrove isn't a genre — it's the operating principle.

What follows is a collection of artefacts from this design universe. These are glimpses of parallel futures built not just to be imagined, but lived into. Also, some of them are just fun.
