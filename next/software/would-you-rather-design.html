Would You Rather Design

A way of finding out what you like in design by choosing between two options, marking up parts of a picture and saying why. Every answer is kept as a record of taste.

In development. A first version runs on our own machines and holds our own answers. We are working through the design in stages before it is built further, and it is not open to others yet.

Who it is for

A designer or client who needs to say what they want and cannot describe it, and a studio that wants its decisions and its research on taste to come from the same record.

People find it hard to describe what they like in design, and easy to say which of two things they prefer. Would You Rather Design starts from that. It shows you pairs, lets you mark the parts of a picture you like or dislike, asks for a reason, and can follow up with a short interview led by an agent. No single answer becomes a rule; the answers add up to a picture of taste that designers and agents can use.

In detail

  1. Choose between two designs and give a reason. Each answer is saved to a local database, and the original answer file is kept first.

  2. Mark up parts of a picture, and the marks are saved as part of the same record.

  3. A design project, run properly: research on twelve earlier feedback tools, a playthrough critique of the current prototype with screenshots, and a product definition in progress.
