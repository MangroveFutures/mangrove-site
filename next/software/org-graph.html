Org graph

A map of an organisation drawn from its own records: who owns what and how the parts connect.

Research prototype. The drawings work on real data. There is no product around them yet: no import for another organisation's records, and no live link to Epoch beyond one embedded view.

Who it is for

Small and medium organisations that cannot say who owns what, and anyone onboarding into a place with many systems and few diagrams.

Organisations are hard to see. The Org graph draws one from the records it already has: people, teams, apps, databases and agents, joined by typed links such as owns, uses, reads and reports to. One view draws the organisation as a building, with a floor for each area, so you can find who works where.

In detail

  1. One graph composed from each part's own architecture model, so the picture is built from the records and not redrawn by hand. The prototype uses Mangrove's own graph of 272 parts and 328 links.

  2. The same graph drawn several ways: an atlas, an organograph that shows the organisation as a building, a board and a terrain. The view is a drawing of the data, never a separate copy.

  3. Shows the pieces of one person's area: everything they own and everything reached from it along owns, uses, reads, writes and reports-to links.

  4. Tested on a real company as well as our own: a published company handbook was drawn as a building to see whether the idea carries over to an organisation we do not run.
