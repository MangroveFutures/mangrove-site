BodyScan

A guided walk through the body, toe to head, for noticing where and how things feel, and a clear summary you can take to an appointment.

Research prototype. A first version has been built and a second set of demos made, and both run in the browser. It is not a medical device and makes no medical claims.

BodyScan is a guided way to describe your body. It takes you through 23 regions from toe to head, asks where something is and what it is like, keeps dated sessions so you can compare over time, and can print a short summary for a doctor. It does not diagnose and does not triage.
