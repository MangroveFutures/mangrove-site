## Who we are
**Decided 13 Oct 2026 (Rosie, in session):**
- **Rosie's own role: minimal for now, a short story.** It must not read as a solo project, without claiming more than is true. Mangrove works with other collaborators, who are unlikely to be listed yet, so About reads as an organisation rather than one person who tries to do everything.
- **The agent team is a feature:** show how the studio works, with humans and named AI agents, plus a "how this site was made" note.
- **Cognitive diversity is a stance of the whole project and organisation,** not accommodations, and never asking people to change to suit software. Say it openly in the sections meant for those readers (About, specific papers, some services), not everywhere, because it could put some people off. Elsewhere the framing is the new era of systems, software and tools that adapt to us and let us be more human.
