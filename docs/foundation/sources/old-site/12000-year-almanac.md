The Human Almanac — a prototype from Mangrove

The Year of Work — A Mangrove Almanac

12,000 years compressed into a single calendar year.
A design experiment in Human General Intelligence — how humans and AI think, imagine, and build together.

How to read this: Each month represents 1,000 years. We end at midnight, 31 Dec 2025 → 1 Jan 2026. December spans 1026–2025 CE (~32 years/day, ~1.3 years/hour). Zoom: full year → December → last week → last day → last hour.

1) The full year (12,000 → 1)

Spring (Mar–May): settlements → proto-cities
Summer (Jun–Aug): writing, cities, empires, pyramids
Autumn (Sep–Nov): iron, philosophy, paper, navigation
Winter (Dec): print → science → industry → computing → AI
