Breathing Room

A breathing practice with a guided timer, water sound and a slow flock to watch. It records each session, and is meant to ask the AI agents to rest while you do.

In development. The breathing page works and runs inside Epoch. Saving sessions to the database, and the agents resting alongside you, are the parts still to build.

Who it is for

People who want a simple, calm breathing practice that records itself, and who work alongside AI and want the machine side to slow down too. It is built first for one person, and is not a medical product.

Breathing Room is a breathing practice built around breath-hold tables, made first for Rosie's own training. A session gives you a calm orb to follow, generated water sound, a slow flock of birds, a cue before each hold and an always-visible stop. The idea behind it is that people and systems both work better with rest, so starting a session is also meant to tell Mangrove's AI agents to stop starting new work and tidy up.

In detail

  1. Three guided breathing tables: a standard breath-hold table, a 2:4 pattern and a plain "just breathe" option, each with a cue one breath before a hold.

  2. Calm by design: generated water sound, a slow flock that nearly stills if you ask for reduced motion, a thin progress line instead of a bar, and a tap anywhere to end a hold early with no penalty.

  3. Records each session at the first breath and finishes it at the end, with CSV and JSON export. At present the record stays in your own browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

  4. Specified, not yet working: sessions saved to a database with the version used, the length and the type, set beside sleep and heart-rate variability; and agents that pause while a session runs. The database tables exist and hold no sessions yet.
