Your health story, in all its glory

In 30–60 minutes, we help you create a complete, structured record of your symptoms, history, medications, reactions, family history, exposures, and hunches — in your own words and on your own terms.

You own it. You can update it anytime.

With consent, it's anonymised and added to a growing library of lived experiences — fuelling research, prevention, and better design in healthcare.

Why it matters

We often have short appointments, with limited time (and sometimes, interest) for clinicians to capture your experience.
We're often worried, pressured, rushed and may not represent our symptoms fully and accurately.
You may see a range of different specialists, or different doctors, often at different locations meaning insights don't get shared.
Many health systems force you to repeat a reduced version of your story until nuance — and sometimes vital clues — disappear.
That leads to missed diagnoses, delayed treatment, and reduced quality of life - especially in complex or multi-system disorders.

We start with lived experience as the primary data, not as an afterthought. This is design-led healthcare R&D: parallel to clinical pathways, not restricted by them.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants:
- More control over their health data
- To feel like they're really being listened to
- To prepare for appointments and emergencies
- To understand family patterns and risks
- To contribute to research without being a passive "case"

What it is

The HealthStory you receive is:
- A clear, structured chronology of your health journey
- A record of medications, reactions, allergies, and exposures
- Your personal theories and observations
- Ready to share with clinicians, family, or keep private
- A living document you can update anytime
