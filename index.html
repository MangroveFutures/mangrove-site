Work
Platform

Epoch

A personal cognitive operating system and knowledge platform. Epoch ingests conversation history across AI tools, structures it as a typed hypergraph, and produces materialised views — notebook, wiki, agent summaries — that stay current without manual effort. Built by Mangrove, used by Mangrove to build itself.

2024– In development
Engine

Choreotope

A murmuration engine for movement and pattern visualisation. Generates emergent flocking behaviour from natural-language descriptions of rhythm, density, and relationship. The visual language of this site comes from Choreotope.

2026 In development
Research

Kronoscope

Hypergraph analysis layer for the Mangrove conversation corpus. Studies the topology of long-running human–AI dialogue — how ideas cluster, which nodes are structurally critical, how the graph evolves over time.

2025– In development
App

WingMan

Group event planning tool. Coordinates RSVPs, logistics, shared information and timing for groups. First instance: a stag do app. Built on Mangrove's platform.

2026 Live
Tool

Mindsweeper

AI conversation analysis and personal knowledge graph ingestion. Extracts patterns, decisions, and relationships from AI chat histories and brings them into the graph. The first step of the Mangrove extraction cascade.

2026 Early access
App

InnPut

Group accommodation research tool. Helps groups find, compare, and coordinate on accommodation options — rooms, prices, proximity, preferences — without the usual spreadsheet chaos.

2026 In development
World / Game

Islands of Doc Tomorrow

A speculative fiction world game. An alternative future, built as a navigable world with its own bibliography, characters, and rules. A long-running creative project alongside the research.

2024– Ongoing
Creative practice

Data art

Work made from real and found datasets — photography, directing, sculpture. A separate practice from the research and tools, kept deliberately distinct.
