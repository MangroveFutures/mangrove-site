# HealthStory AI product page (draft for Rosie)

Size of job: small, a product page on the new site (`next/`). Copy, colour and placement are Rosie's to decide; this is a draft.

## Brief
Rosie, 20 Nov 2026: HealthStory AI needs a page on the new site, linked and visible now; placement and branding can change. Public repo, so only the idea and the product are described, nothing from participants or interviews.

## Information architecture
Software list (`next/software.html`) has three entries: the Idea column, the one-line list, and the Spin-outs line. ## Process
- Started from Mangrove's own material: `next/software/breathing-room.html` (structure, nav, tokens, status mark, pager) and `next/site.css`. No new CSS file; a small page-local style block.
- Took the looks from the private product repo (bodyscan-chart, healthstory-chart): the Gray hand plate, the Nightingale plate, and the solid, dashed or dotted line key. Only public-domain images, credited.
- Two directions were not built: this is one page in an existing system, so the layout follows the site. The one choice made was the status mark: Idea (open ring), because designs exist and nothing is built.
- Skills: impeccable shape (restraint, no cards, no pills), accessibility (axe in a scratch folder, not added to the repo).
- Defaults check: no cream ground, italic serif or card grid added; the engraving is the picture. Honest answer: the page is the site's own look, so it is only as distinctive as the site.

## Checks
Rendered at 390 and 1280 px, dark and light; viewed. axe: no violations from this page's own content. The only two reports (colour contrast and label mismatch) are on the shared accessibility kit button the site injects on every page, including Breathing Room, so they are not new. Body text is 16px or more; the status labels use the site's existing small caps style. No sideways scroll.
