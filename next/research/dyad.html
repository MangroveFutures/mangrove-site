The human–AI dyad

A person and their AI collaborators, treated as one system that learns as its parts learn each other.

Go deeper

The unit of study

Mangrove studies the pair, not the person alone and not the model alone. A person and their AI collaborators are treated as one regulating system, and the question is what that system does that neither part does alone.

The record

The evidence is a long dialogue between a person and AI, kept as a conversation record covering about two years. It is being turned into a shared memory that both partners can use, and into a world model: a maintained account of how the pair thinks.

Tools as instruments

The apps Mangrove builds double as research instruments. Client work funds the research, and what is learned goes back into the tools.

The questions

  1. What does a person-and-AI pair do, as one regulating system, that neither does alone?
  2. Can a long human–AI conversation record become a shared memory and world model that both partners can use, and how do we tell it is working?
